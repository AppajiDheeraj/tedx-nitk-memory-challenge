The signal board

How to play

Find the signal.

  1. Flip two cards. Tap any two cards to turn them over. If the symbols match, they stay open. If not, they flip back — remember where they were.
  2. Clear the board in 5:00. Find every pair before the timer runs out. The clock keeps running even while this window is open.
  3. Climb three levels. Easy (8 pairs) unlocks Medium (10 pairs), which unlocks Hard (15 pairs). Finish any level to reveal this year's confirmed speakers.
  4. Score. Every two cards you flip is one move. Fewest moves wins; time breaks ties. Only your best run on each level counts, so replay as often as you like.
  5. Win. The top Hard players win prizes — we'll contact them by email. To continue later, enter the same email and username.
